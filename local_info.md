This workshop will take place at the Fairfield Inn & Suites Santa Cruz - Capitola (1255 41st Ave, Capitola, CA 95010) from Oct. 28th to Nov. 1st.

The closest airport to the greater Santa Cruz area is the San José Mineta International Airport (SJC). We will be organizing shuttles to get to the venue on October 27th (the day before the start of the workshop).

![Driving route from San Jose airport to the venue, about 35 miles](/assets/images/route-sjc-to-venue.png)

The trip from SJC to the hotel is about 35 miles and the commute typically takes 45 minutes when traffic is normal.
The route from SJC to the hotel passes through Highway 17, which is a well-known windy road through the redwoods.

![Detail of the route through Santa Cruz via Highway 1 and 41st Ave](/assets/images/route-santa-cruz-detail.png)

The hotel is located in the Capitola/Pleasure Point area, on the eastern side of Santa Cruz county.
Whereas Santa Cruz is more of a beachy-city, Capitola is more of a tourist resort.
There are stores, shops, and restaurants in walking distance to the hotel, but to go much further (e.g. into Santa Cruz or to UC Santa Cruz), you would need to drive or find a ride.

In late October the weather in the Santa Cruz area is often mild, chilly, or cold. It could be good to bring a lighter and a thicker layer of clothing, especially if you'd like to go to the beach.
